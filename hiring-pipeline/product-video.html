Core Features

Built for Modern Recruiting

Four pillars that transform your hiring workflow.

🤖
AI-Powered CV Scoring
Claude AI evaluates candidates across 5 dimensions — role relevance, experience, location, compensation, and culture fit — delivering a 0-100 score instantly.
💬
Pre-Interview Briefings
AI-driven conversational briefing ensures candidates understand the role before interviews. Evaluates understanding and genuine interest automatically.
🏆
Consultant Rankings
Data-driven consultant performance tracking with yield rates, time-to-fill, and auto-ranking by role family. Always assign the best-fit recruiter.
📅
Smart Scheduling
Candidates book interviews via unique token links — no login required. Slot management prevents double-booking and streamlines coordination.
